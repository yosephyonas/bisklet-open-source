# Third-party components

Flutter/Dart, Laravel/PHP, Go tooling, mapping providers and other dependencies have licenses and terms separate from Apache License 2.0 for Bisklet's original source.

The previously bundled PubNub SDK has been removed. Four third-party location photographs referenced by the private website are excluded and replaced with nonphotographic placeholders.

Before deploying integrations, review vendor protocol rights, map service terms and municipal permissions.
